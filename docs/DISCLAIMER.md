# Disclaimer baseline

Thailand Disaster Watch is intended as a situation-monitoring and decision-support platform. It is not a government agency, an official public-warning system, or a substitute for instructions from competent authorities.

The platform cannot guarantee continuity, completeness, accuracy, or timeliness of third-party services. Calculated indicators and summaries must not be represented as official warnings. AI, if introduced in a later approved phase, may summarize only data actually available to the system and must not invent measurements, predictions, or thresholds.

Production wording, legal review, Thai/English translations, owner identity, contact channels, and jurisdiction-specific terms require human confirmation before launch.
