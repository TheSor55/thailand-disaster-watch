## 2. External Data Providers & Attribution

All meteorological, hydrological, and seismic data feeds rendered within the platform remain the intellectual property of their respective originating authorities:

1. **Thai Meteorological Department (TMD / กรมอุตุนิยมวิทยา):**
   * *Data:* Weather observations, storm tracks, and official public weather advisories.
2. **Royal Irrigation Department (RID / กรมชลประทาน):**
   * *Data:* Reservoir capacity, inflow, and hourly outflow telemetry.
3. **Hydro-Informatics Institute (HII / สถาบันสารสนเทศทรัพยากรน้ำ - สสน. / ThaiWater):**
   * *Data:* National hydrological telemetry, river gauging stations, and Chao Phraya Basin flow diagrams.
4. **Geo-Informatics and Space Technology Development Agency (GISTDA):**
   * *Data:* Sentinel-1 SAR satellite flood extent and space geoinformatics.
5. **Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation (DDPM / กรมป้องกันและบรรเทาสาธารณภัย - ปภ.):**
   * *Data:* Disaster situation reports and provincial hazard mitigation portals.
6. **United States Geological Survey (USGS) & EMSC:**
   * *Data:* Global seismic activity and earthquake epicenter catalogs.
7. **RainViewer & Open-Meteo:**
   * *Data:* Global weather radar composite tiles and numerical weather prediction forecasts.
